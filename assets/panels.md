- estuaries have a lot of plant and animal life (like, honestly, just a ton of life in general because this is where all of the organic matter that sustains ecosystems is there to fester)

- the ending is where the girl has run off far past her own house where the river meets the sea at an estuary
- you cna choose to sink into the waters of the estuary and go swimming to let yourself feel alive again with your best friend pulling you forward, or you can choose to slink back until your best friend drags you forward just to make you feel a bit of life again, finally
